## Implemented boundary

- [Startup](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:128) takes canonical-root disk admission before scan, intent read/decision/replay/quarantine, delegation reconciliation and watcher scheduling; [migration](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:875) takes admission before the store mutex. The [shared repair](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:639) requires it. No parent admission is acquired within a store mutex or task/API lock.
- [Watcher and timer registration](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:152) is detached from the startup async context so later unrelated reconciliation cannot inherit an already released admission lease.
- [Binding](../../src/core/task-persistence/targetPersistenceBoundary.ts:118) takes admission and the target parent lease before sampling identity/API and publishing; it refuses outstanding or quarantined intent evidence, checks both lease generations before and after publication and does not grant B1 eligibility. An already-started intent rename makes binding refuse rather than succeeding before the effect.
- Malformed intent checks [persisted binding evidence](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:713) rather than only cache entries; [intent effects](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:788) and [task-file callbacks](../../src/core/task-persistence/TaskHistoryStore.ts:951) check the active startup generation. A stolen admission or uncertain evidence fails closed; another parent's startup repair still completes.
- The physical intent-rename interleaving, reverse order, two stores, bound parent missing from cache, malformed/valid intents, generation loss before rename and binding publication, root alias and pre-binding file-lock work are covered in [startup tests](../../src/core/task-persistence/__tests__/manualSupersedeB1.startupQuarantine.spec.ts:1) and [boundary tests](../../src/core/task-persistence/__tests__/targetPersistenceBoundary.spec.ts:1). A successful binding followed by targeted quarantine is **not** an accepted observation.
